About

A place to relearn movement —
Gabby Studio

Gabby Studio is more than a workout space. It is a movement rehabilitation studio where you restore the balance and function of your body. We combine Pilates with GYROTONIC® and GYROKINESIS®, methods built on spiraling and circular motion, to wake up deep muscles without straining the joints and to work through pain from its root cause.

Equipment

Our Equipment

GYROTONIC® equipment can only be purchased and taught by instructors who have completed official certification training. Gabby Studio is equipped with both the core machine and special equipment.

GYROTONIC®

Core machine

Pulley Tower

The foundational GYROTONIC® machine. Following pulleys and rotating wheels, the spine moves in circles and spirals, building range of motion and strength together.

Special equipment · NEW

Archway

At 95 inches (about 2.4 m), the tallest GYROTONIC® machine. Its vertical ladder structure allows vertical, horizontal and spiral movement as well as diagonals and inversions, progressing step by step from beginner to advanced.

Special equipment

Jumping-Stretching Board

A low-impact machine for running and jumping in a horizontal plane on gliding slides and rotating discs, with the spine and pelvis supported. Effective for whole-body balance, flexibility, muscle tone and core strength.

Special equipment

Leg Extension Unit

A patented cam lets the unit lengthen as the leg extends, so hips and knees move without compression in full-body sequences that coordinate upper and lower body, hands and feet.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

I've never exercised before. Is that okay?

Yes. Every program starts at your level after an initial consultation and movement assessment, so no prior experience is needed.

How is GYROTONIC® different from Pilates?

Pilates focuses on core stabilization and alignment, while GYROTONIC® moves the spine and joints three-dimensionally through continuous circular and spiral motion. At Gabby Studio we use both, tailored to your condition.

Can I exercise after surgery?

Yes, once your medical team has cleared you for exercise. Tell us about your surgical history and current condition and we will recommend a safe program for your stage of recovery.

How do I book and check prices?

All sessions and consultations are by appointment, and consultations are held in person at the studio. Please ask about fees and times via KakaoTalk open chat. It is hard to answer calls during sessions, so messages are preferred. Consultations are mainly in Korean.
